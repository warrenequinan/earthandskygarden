Earth and Sky Garden
Client

Earth and Sky Garden is a farm and produce business that offers fresh vegetables, fruits, meat, and poultry products. This project is being developed as a custom e-commerce platform to improve their online presence and provide customers with a more convenient way to browse and purchase products.

Project Overview

Earth and Sky Garden is an e-commerce platform designed to showcase and sell farm-fresh products online. The platform aims to provide a simple and user-friendly shopping experience while giving administrators the tools needed to manage products, inventory, and customer orders efficiently.

Development Notes

This project is being built with a focus on maintainability, scalability, and a seamless shopping experience. The architecture separates the customer-facing website from the administration dashboard while utilizing Supabase as the backend service for data management and authentication.
